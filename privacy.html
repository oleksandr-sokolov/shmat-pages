Who we are

Shmat: Cut the Exact Piece (“Shmat”, “the app”) is made by Oleksandr Sokolov, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: berry.raspin@gmail.com.

Account and sign-in

On first launch the app creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication. It is a random user ID and contains no name or email.

You can optionally sign in with Google or Apple to keep your progress across devices. Firebase Authentication then stores the account identifier and the email address that Google or Apple return for you. If you use Apple's “Hide My Email”, we receive only the relay address Apple creates. We use this information only to sign you in.

Game data and leaderboards

The app stores the following in Cloud Firestore, linked to your user ID:

  • a nickname, generated automatically, which you can change;
  • daily results: the cut path you drew, your scores, and the time from opening the shape to your best cut;
  • monthly totals, your streak, and your endless-mode best score.

Game requests (for example, submitting a cut) are processed by Cloud Functions on Google Cloud. Your nickname and scores are shown publicly on the daily, monthly and endless-mode leaderboards to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one — for +3 attempts at the daily shape or +1 life in endless mode;
  • interstitial ads between rounds in endless mode.

Ads are requested as non-personalized, which means they are not based on a profile of you. AdMob may still use device identifiers, including the advertising ID, and information such as your IP address for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded, where required.

Analytics and crash reports

  • Firebase Analytics records usage events such as opening the daily challenge, making a cut, completing the day, sharing a result and watching an ad, together with an app-instance identifier and basic device information (model, OS version, approximate country).
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: the stack trace, device model, OS version and an installation identifier.

We use these only to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device when a new shape is ready. It is created on the device itself; we do not run a push-notification server and do not collect a push token.

Data on your device

Your settings and a cached copy of the current day are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

The app has no payments and does not access your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Where data is stored

Game data in Cloud Firestore is stored in Google Cloud region europe-west1 (Belgium). Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob are global Google services and may process data in the United States and other countries. For transfers outside the EEA and the UK, Google relies on safeguards such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone other than the providers listed above.

How long we keep data

Your account, nickname and results are kept while your account exists. Leaderboards are rebuilt from results, so when your account is deleted you disappear from them too. Analytics events and crash reports are kept by Firebase for its standard retention periods.

Legal bases and your rights

We process account and game data because it is needed to provide the game you use (performance of a contract). We process analytics, crash reports and ad measurement under our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and free. Where the law requires consent (for example, for ads in the EEA and the UK), we ask for it through the consent form.

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for access to your data, a copy, correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and your data from our servers. It cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to berry.raspin@gmail.com and include your nickname. To make sure the account is yours, we will reply with a short code and ask you to set it as your nickname in the app for a moment; then we delete the account for you.

Children

Shmat is rated for ages 12 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The font is loaded from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
